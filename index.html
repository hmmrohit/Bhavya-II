Hi, I'm Bhavya Sharma — a dancer, choreographer, performing arts facilitator, and theatre enthusiast.

From leading cultural spaces to choreographing award-winning performances and conducting workshops for educators and students alike, I strive to create art that inspires, communicates, and brings people together. My journey spans classical dance forms, contemporary movement, and community theatre — each discipline shaping a holistic artistic voice.
